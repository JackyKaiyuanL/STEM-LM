VARIABLES AND DAILY AGGREGATION  (extract_era5_at_points.py)
------------------------------------------------------------
For every (latitude, longitude, date) row the extractor loads the month that
contains the date, aggregates hourly values to UTC calendar days, and samples
the nearest grid cell and day:

  env_t2m_min   daily minimum of 2m_temperature        (K)
  env_t2m_max   daily maximum of 2m_temperature        (K)
  env_t2m_mean  daily mean of 2m_temperature           (K)
  env_tp_sum    daily sum of total_precipitation       (m)

Temperatures are lapse-rate corrected to the site elevation:
  t_site = t_era5 + (z_era5 − z_dem) × 0.0065 K/m
where z_dem is sampled from the Copernicus GLO-30 DEM
(../copernicus_dem/COP30_hh_vsicurl.vrt) and z_era5 is the ERA5 invariant
surface geopotential divided by g = 9.80665 m/s². The geopotential is
downloaded once from the Copernicus CDS (reanalysis-era5-single-levels,
variable geopotential, 2020-01-01 00:00; requires ~/.cdsapirc) and cached at
~/.cache/era5_orog_na.nc, because the in-store surface geopotential is NaN
over land.
